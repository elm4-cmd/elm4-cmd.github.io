PRIVACY POLICY — SUMMON TRAILS TD

Last updated: 7 November 2026

1. About this policy

This policy covers Summon Trails TD for Android, package com.elm_cr4.summontrails. It explains how the game uses information on your device. The game works offline and does not require a user account.

2. Information kept on your device

The game saves your campaign progress, scores and records, unlocked defenders and items, chosen Defender Deck, cosmetic selections, Realm Coins balance and reward/purchase history, and language, audio, haptic and tutorial preferences.

It also saves checkpoints of an active expedition so that you can continue after an interruption. These include the map and game rules, wave, army, enemies, cards, abilities, timers, random-generator state and camera position needed to restore the match.

Weekly Challenge uses the device's date, time and time-zone information to select the week, display dates and limit repeated rewards. Weekly maps, records and history remain local; there is no online leaderboard.

Random match identifiers and local transaction records prevent duplicate results or rewards. These are game records, not advertising identifiers or identifiers read from your device.

3. Why the game uses this information

Local information lets the game remember your preferences and progress, apply unlocks and cosmetics, maintain records and Realm Coins, and restore an expedition. Touch and screen-layout information is used to operate the controls. It is not sent to the developer.

4. No automatic collection or sharing by the game

The game has no Internet permission and does not automatically send saved games, gameplay statistics, device identifiers or other user information to the developer or to a server. It contains no advertising, analytics or cloud crash-reporting SDK, no real-money purchases, no user accounts and no social or multiplayer service.

Realm Coins are earned and spent inside the game; they are not purchased with real money. The game does not access your contacts, location, camera or microphone.

5. Local diagnostics and third-party components

The game uses Godot Engine and Android components for graphics, sound, input and local storage. Technical startup, rendering or error messages may be written to the device's system logs. Debug and test builds can log additional gameplay and performance details. The game does not upload these logs.

Android, the device manufacturer and Google Play may independently handle installation, updates, system diagnostics or device transfer under their own settings and policies. The game has no developer-operated cloud save. Android cloud backup is disabled for the app, but some manufacturers may still support device-to-device transfer. Copies made by device or system tools are controlled by those tools, not by the developer.

6. Storage, security and deletion

Saved data stays in the app's local storage until it is replaced through normal play or removed. The game does not separately encrypt saved files. Android's app-storage protections and your device's security settings apply.

You can remove local game data through Android Settings > Apps > Summon Trails TD > Storage > Clear data/storage, or by uninstalling the game. Menu names vary by device. This permanently removes local progress, Realm Coins, unlocks and expedition recovery on that device. There is no developer cloud copy to restore or delete remotely. Any device-transfer or system-managed copies must be managed using the relevant device or service controls.

7. Contact and voluntary support messages

For privacy questions, contact support.elmcr4@gmail.com.

If you choose to email support or send a bug report, your mail provider sends the message and the details you include. They are handled outside the game to reply to your request and kept only as reasonably needed to address it or meet applicable obligations. Avoid sending sensitive information, especially personal information about children. You can ask the contact address about deleting your correspondence. The game does not automatically attach logs or saved files.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated if the game's data practices change. The updated text and date will be included in the game and in its published privacy policy. Any future network, advertising, account or analytics feature would require a new review and appropriate disclosures.
