{{APP_NAME}} is provided by Emil Lindström (elm_cr4), Sweden. For privacy questions, contact support.elmcr4@gmail.com.

Information used on your device

The app stores information you choose to enter, including workouts, sets, goals, notes, exercises, programs, equipment, timers, settings, selected exercise images and optional body measurements. These records support your training log and local progress views. This release works without an app account and does not provide account sync, community sharing, purchases, advertising or Health Connect access.

Images and timer signals

You can select exercise images through Android's photo picker. Notification permission is used for timer notifications. If you enable spoken timer cues, your device's text-to-speech engine handles the timer text; the app looks for an installed voice marked as offline. Sound and vibration follow your chosen timer settings.

Files you choose to export

When you export CSV or ZIP, you choose the destination through Android's file picker. That destination may be a cloud provider or another location outside your device. ZIP can optionally include your own original exercise images; bundled third-party exercise images are not exported.

The export covers documented content and is not a complete restorable backup. This version cannot import an exported file. Keep exported files private if they contain training notes or body measurements. An interrupted export may leave an incomplete file at the chosen destination.

Android settings backup

If Android backup or device transfer is enabled, Android may copy selected app, timer and progression settings. This selection is limited to 30 settings: 13 app settings, 6 timer-cue settings and 11 progression-feedback settings.

This settings document excludes workout history, body measurements and app identities. The backup provider and device settings control this system process. Some settings may return after reinstalling the app or moving to another device. This does not restore your training history.

Storage and removal

Records are kept in Android app-private storage. Removing a workout from the visible history is not secure erasure of every retained revision or recovery record. Android's app-storage controls can remove the app's local data.

Exported files and system-managed backup copies are separate; manage those copies through their storage or backup provider. Removing the app's local data does not delete files you exported elsewhere.

Support messages

If you contact us, we receive the information you include in your message. Avoid sending training exports or body measurements unless you choose to share information needed to explain your issue.

Privacy requests

You can contact support.elmcr4@gmail.com about personal information held in support correspondence, including requests for access, correction or deletion. Other rights may apply under data protection law. These requests do not give the developer remote access to training records kept only on your device.

You may also contact Sweden's Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) about your data protection rights or a complaint.

Visiting these information pages

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub privacy statement for GitHub's handling of website visits. This website-hosting process is separate from the app's local training records.

Changes and questions

The policy date identifies the current published text. If a later release adds online services, its data handling needs updated information. Contact support.elmcr4@gmail.com about this policy or your support correspondence.
