Huge - Workout tracker

An Android app for offline workout logging, programs, training history and customisable timers.

This release works without an app account. The app interface and these information pages are in English.

Privacy and help

  • Read the privacy policy
    How local records, selected images, timer signals, exports and Android settings backup are handled.
  • Get support
    Contact details, issue-reporting guidance and answers about export and changing phones.

Local records and exported files

Training records are stored on your device. You can export documented content as CSV or ZIP to a destination you choose. Export coverage is partial, and this version cannot import those files.

Android may back up selected settings. That settings backup does not include workout history.
