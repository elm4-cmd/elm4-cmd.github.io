Document Viewer

Document Viewer opens PDFs, JPEG and PNG images, static WebP images and plain text on Android. Open supported files from Android's file picker or Open with menu. Scan documents, combine images into a PDF or save a PDF page as a PNG.

In the app, choose Open document for a read-only Office preview. Word (.docx) shows text, lists and simple tables. Excel (.xlsx) shows cell addresses and stored values in visible sheets. PowerPoint (.pptx) shows text from visible slides in presentation order.

The free version may show ads depending on your privacy choices and ad availability. An optional one-time Premium purchase removes ads.
