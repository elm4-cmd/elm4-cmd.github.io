1. Service purpose

Bridge: Closed Test ("Bridge") coordinates Android closed testing between developers and testers. It helps users discover apps to test, record eligible testing contributions, list their own apps, and take part in structured Campaigns.

Bridge is an independent service. Bridge is not Google and is not endorsed by Google. Bridge cannot represent Google, change Google Play requirements, or guarantee that Google Play will approve an app, release, or closed test.

Your public Bridge alias must not impersonate another person or organization, mislead other users, or contain abusive, discriminatory, unlawful, or rights-infringing content.

3. Genuine testing behavior

Bridge is intended for genuine testing contributions. Testers should install and use eligible apps as instructed, provide honest completion information, and respect app owners and other participants.

Bridge does not reward or require Google Play ratings or reviews. Coin rewards relate to eligible testing contribution—not the content, score, or existence of a Play Store rating or review. Users must not pressure others to leave positive ratings or reviews or make a reward conditional on doing so.

4. Test listings

An active Test listing currently runs for 48 hours. A base listing may require Coins. Permanent Play slot upgrades may provide free listing capacity, while a temporary slot cost may apply when no included capacity is available.

Current prices, available balance, capacity, and fees are shown in Bridge before confirmation. Once a listing is activated, applicable Coin fees are not refunded merely because the user cancels or stops using the listing early. Listing availability, prices, capacity, and eligibility remain subject to the current Bridge policy displayed in the app.

5. Campaigns

Under the current Campaign rules:

  • a Campaign has 16 participant slots and begins when all 16 slots are filled;
  • entry Coins are reserved while the Campaign is waiting to fill;
  • the reservation is released if a participant leaves before the Campaign starts;
  • the reservation becomes spent when the Campaign starts;
  • the Campaign runs through 14 rolling 24-hour periods;
  • participants are expected to complete the required testing work during each period;
  • warnings may be issued for missed required work, and a third warning removes the participant;
  • there is no Coin refund after Campaign start or removal under the current Campaign rules; and
  • Campaign work itself currently grants 0 Coins.

Bridge shows the applicable commitment and entry cost before a participant joins. Campaign rules may be updated for future Campaigns; the rules displayed at confirmation govern that participation, subject to applicable law.

6. Coins

Coins are an in-app participation currency. They are not money or cryptocurrency, have no cash value, are not transferable outside Bridge, and cannot be withdrawn or redeemed for money.

Coins may be earned, reserved, spent, withheld, or reversed under current Bridge policies. Bridge may correct Coin state where fraud, reward abuse, duplicate grants, technical errors, chargebacks, or other invalid activity are identified. Coin operations are server-authoritative.
