Your account and associated data

Delete your Bridge account

Deleting a Bridge account concerns Bridge data. It does not delete your Google account. Uninstalling Bridge does not delete its server account.

Information updated: October 16, 2026

Current availability

Account-deletion requests are handled manually by Bridge's developer. Email support.elmcr4@gmail.com and state that you want your Bridge account and associated personal data deleted. The developer verifies account ownership before taking action and replies manually. Sending an email is not confirmation that deletion has been completed.

This page opens an email draft only. It does not send a request or change your account, Coins or purchases.

1. Contact the developer

Email from the Google email address you use to sign in to Bridge and say that you want to close your Bridge account and delete its associated personal data. You do not need to install or open Bridge to make contact.

support.elmcr4@gmail.com

The link includes the request below in Swedish. Review the draft and send it yourself. If no email app opens, copy the address and wording into your email service.

Do not email passwords, sign-in tokens, Play purchase tokens, payment card details or private purchase receipts.

2. Ownership and manual handling

The developer reviews your request and replies manually by email. A reply acknowledging receipt does not establish account ownership or confirm completed deletion. No separate support portal or case account is required.

Before any closure, the operator must verify control of the existing account through private confirmation sent to its already verified sign-in address. An email sender field, alias or screenshot alone is not proof of ownership. If you no longer control that address, explain this to the developer; deletion cannot proceed until ownership is securely verified.

3. Coins, purchases and commitments

Valid existing commitments must be reconciled before closure. This includes Coin balances and reservations, pending purchases, permanent Test slot rights, existing Test or Campaign commitments, and any amount owed to another participant. Campaign participation remains paused and rewarded advertising is disabled; that does not cancel valid existing rights or obligations.

After closure, that account's Coins and Bridge rights cannot be used there. If any value, purchased right or uncertain purchase remains, support must provide a private, itemised list and record your informed final case outcome before completion.

A deletion request does not automatically refund, forfeit or transfer Coins or purchases to another account. Google Play purchase and statutory consumer rights remain applicable. No refund or transfer is promised by this page.

4. What must be handled

The verified case plan must cover the Bridge sign-in account, profile and alias, app listings and uploaded icons, Test and Campaign participation, private feedback and reports, notification registrations, and account-linked economic records. Data concerning other participants must be protected.

Before closure, support must identify what will be deleted, which limited records need to remain, the actual reason for retaining each record and the verified condition for removing it. Necessary purchase replay protection, unresolved obligations and the minimal closure record must not be confused with permission to keep unrelated account data indefinitely.

The same plan must address support mail, logs, exports, backups, recoverable database copies, stored icon versions and soft-deleted copies. A restored copy must have the accepted deletions and account protections reapplied before access is reopened. Current provider and backup handling conditions have not yet been verified; no fixed deletion or retention period is promised here.

This page cannot remotely erase an offline device. Confirmed server closure and the clearing of local data managed by Bridge must be checked when the app next connects.

5. Completion and follow-up

Keep your email correspondence with the developer. Only a final confirmation based on verified account closure and associated data handling establishes completion. It must state the actual outcome, the data removed, any necessary retained records and their removal conditions.

If a case is blocked, support must identify the unresolved item and the actual next review or action. An email, a case number, a support reply or the removal of sign-in alone is not a completed deletion.
